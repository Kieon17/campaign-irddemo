1. How To Use This Document

Purpose & Value of this Document

Historically, campaign planning and execution required navigating multiple disjointed files.

The Integrated Requirements Document (IRD) is a unified, single-source-of-truth file. It centralizes strategy, creative execution, technical build, and post-launch monitoring. By maintaining this single document, we eliminate version control issues, prevent data and logic gaps, and streamline the stakeholder sign-off process. It may not remove all the previous documentation or tools but will act as a final receipt throughout the project.

How to Use the Document (Section-by-Section)

SectionWho Owns ItPurposeAction Required
1. Agency Response BriefStrategistsSets the foundation.Outline the business problem, target audience, core strategic approach, and high-level campaign recipes. Fill this out first to align the team.
2. Creative & AssetsDesigners, CopywritersActs as the bridge between copy/design and development.Insert links to approved Brand Share assets, embed final email/push templates, or insert creative recipes directly here.
3. Business Requirements & RulesProject Managers & ArchitectsTranslates strategy into concrete rules.Define entry/exit criteria, journey cadence, segment volume estimates, and taxonomy. Fill out Touchpoint Criteria completely.
4. Solution Requirements (SRS)Solution ArchitectsThe technical blueprint.Map out data flows, integrations (MuleSoft, SFMC), push cadences, and dynamic content rules. Detail deduplication/exclusions.
5. Solution ImplementationApplication ConsultantThe "as-built" record of the campaign.Paste actual SQL automation logic, Data Extension names, and screenshots of Journey Builder canvas, schedules, and segmentation.
6. HypercareApplication ConsultantPost-launch monitoring.Log initial campaign sends, verify tracking logs (Send Logs, Consumer IDs, UTM tags), and monitor for anomalies.
7. LearningsData Insights ConsultantPost-launch learnings.Log learnings for future program enhancements or business learnings that can inform future marketing strategies.
